Our story

It started with a first build at nine.

I built my first PC with my brother when I was nine. It showed me how good a machine feels when it's tuned exactly to what you need, something a factory prebuilt can't give you.

Off-the-shelf PCs force compromises. You overspend on features you'll never use and end up short on the parts that matter. A CAD professional buys a prebuilt with too much storage and a graphics card that bottlenecks the work.

CustomPC.tech fixes that. You tell us what you do, and we put the budget where it counts: processing, rendering, graphics or I/O. Nothing extra, nothing missing.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you really design around my workload?

Yes. We start with your real use cases: apps, games, resolution and noise tolerance. Then we choose hardware based on reliable benchmarks.

How do you validate performance?

We run standardized stress tests for CPU, GPU, thermals and noise, and include the report with your system.

What's included in build verification?

Cable management, thermal validation, burn-in, BIOS settings and stability logs.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Every build is designed with a clear upgrade path for GPU, RAM and storage.

How do you ship?

Double-boxed, with expanding foam inside the case to protect the components.

How long does a build take?

About a week, depending on the build and part availability.
